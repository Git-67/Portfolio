Cookies on this site

The portfolio code sets no cookies and includes no advertising or analytics tools.

How the theme switch works

The site starts with your device’s light or dark setting. If you choose a different theme, the site saves “light” or “dark” in your browser’s session storage under portfolio.theme. This keeps the theme consistent as you move between pages in the same tab.

Clearing your choice

Switch back to your device’s theme to remove the saved setting. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser.
