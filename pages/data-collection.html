Data collection

Page requests

When you open a page, your browser requests its text, images, fonts and scripts from the web host. The host receives connection details such as your IP address and the requested file. Any server logs and their retention depend on the hosting provider.

On your device

The carousels keep track of the current slide while the page is open. The portfolio does not send your slide choices to an analytics service. Your theme choice can be stored in the tab, as described in the cookie policy.

Information you send

Browsing requires no name or email address. If you decide to email me, your mail provider sends the message and any attachments you include.
